# Pool Deck Support

Need help with Pool Deck? Email us at:

**jeff@peg-land.com**

## Report a problem

Please include:

- A description of the problem.
- Your iPhone or iPad model and iOS version.
- The Pool Deck app version, if available.
- Steps to reproduce the problem.
- A screenshot, if helpful, with personal information hidden.

Never send passwords, verification codes, payment details, or private
team footage.

## Account access

For sign-in or password-reset issues, contact us from the email
address associated with your account whenever possible.

## Account deletion and privacy

To request account deletion or ask a privacy question, email us with
the subject “Account deletion” or “Privacy request.”

We may need to verify your identity before processing a request.
Do not send your password.

## Subscriptions and refunds

Manage or cancel your subscription on your iPhone under:

Settings → your name → Subscriptions → Pool Deck

Deleting the app or your Pool Deck account does not cancel an
Apple subscription.

For eligible Apple purchase refunds, visit:
https://reportaproblem.apple.com/

## Report inappropriate content

Use the in-app reporting feature where available, or contact support.
Include enough information to identify the content without forwarding
sensitive footage.
