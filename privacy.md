Pool Deck helps users manage water polo teams, rosters, schedules, game statistics, and team videos.
Information we collect
Depending on the features you use, we collect:
- Account information, such as your email address and account identifier.
- Team information you enter, including player names, cap numbers, schedules, and game statistics.
- Videos, live streams, reports, and other content you submit.
- Subscription status and transaction identifiers used to verify purchases. Apple processes in-app payments; we do not receive your full payment-card details.
- Technical and security information needed to operate and protect the service.
Camera and microphone
Features that record or broadcast video may request camera and microphone access. You control these permissions through your device settings. You must obtain any permissions required to record or share footage of other people.
How we use information
We use information to provide the app, authenticate users, manage team access, store content, verify subscriptions, respond to support requests, investigate reports, and protect the service.
Sharing
Team information and videos may be accessible to team owners and other users with access under the app’s permissions. Users who download or share content may create copies outside Pool Deck.
We use service providers, including Supabase for account authentication and Cloudflare for hosting, storage, and enabled streaming features. Apple handles in-app purchases. We may also disclose information when legally required or reasonably necessary to protect users and the service.
We do not sell personal information or use it for targeted advertising.
Children and youth teams
Pool Deck accounts are intended for adults, including coaches, team administrators, and parents or guardians. Children under 13 may not create accounts.
Adults who enter information about minors or upload their footage must have the necessary authority and permissions. Do not submit unnecessary sensitive information about players. Contact us if you believe a child’s information was submitted without appropriate authorization.
Retention and deletion
We retain information while needed to provide the service and for legitimate security, legal, and operational purposes. You can request account deletion through email. 
Deletion may take time to complete across our providers. Limited records may remain where necessary to prevent abuse, meet legal obligations, or manage purchases. Copies downloaded or shared by other users may remain outside our control.
Deleting your account does not cancel an Apple subscription. Manage subscriptions through your Apple account.
Your choices and rights
You can manage device permissions, request account deletion, and contact us about access to or correction of your information. Depending on where you live, you may have additional privacy rights.
Security and international processing
We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no service is completely secure. Our providers may process information outside your country, subject to applicable legal requirements.
Changes
We may update this policy. We will post the revised policy and effective date and provide additional notice when required.
